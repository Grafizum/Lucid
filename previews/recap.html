09New today

Every language, and a phone that speaks for itself

The picker went from 20 hand-picked targets to all 60 languages ML Kit can hold a model for, with a search field that folds accents (“espanol” finds Español). With nothing chosen yet, Lucid translates into the phone’s own language instead of assuming English, and the model-tag bridges (iw↔he, fil↔tl, zh‑TW↔zh) are handled so detection and the picker always agree. Lucid’s own shell — notification, quick button, text-access name — is now localised in 20 languages.
