Five loops, no standing circle

Every option below drops the always-on outer ring. They run at the real speed (4.4 s a cycle) and the real faintness — all of them are meant to be felt at arm’s length, not watched. Tell me the letter and it goes on the phone.

ABreath

What you have now, minus the frame. One soft ring leaves the mark, widens a third of the way out and lets go halfway. Nothing on screen when it is at rest.

BRipple

Two rings, half a cycle apart. A slow sonar: the second leaves as the first is halfway out, so there is always one faint ring travelling, never a standing one.

CReticle

A third of a circle tracing round. The arc sweeps past the mark and dissolves on the far side, like a lens finding focus. Quietest of the three with a circle.

DGlass

No ring at all. A warm wash comes forward behind the mark while the mark itself pushes 3.5 % closer and gives up a tenth of its weight — the zoom you asked for, carried by light.

EBare

Nothing but the mark. A 3.5 % push and a 10 % fade, once every 4.4 s. For when the page should be completely still and only the lens is alive.

All five use the same 4.4 s cycle and the same accent at 22–30 % opacity, so whatever is chosen sits at the same weight as the rest of the app. Pick a letter — or a mix, e.g. “C’s arc with D’s wash”.
