04language sheetopen

A map of the languages you already carry.

The title becomes the dotted field from your image — doing work instead of decorating: one clay pin for every language whose words are downloaded, so the picture answers “how much of the world does this phone speak” before a word is read. Chips of the last three sit under it, above the search.

CostThe pins need coordinates for a few hundred languages, and the field has to stay honest for right-to-left scripts. That data does not exist yet — this is the one of the three that cannot ship this week.
