Below are three ways to put A beside it. Every mark on this page is drawn at the size the row actually uses, in the same 24-unit box, with the same 1.5-unit hairline — so what you see is the pixel size, not a scaled-up guess.

What 15 dp costs

The row gives the mark 15 dp, so each of two glyphs gets about 6.5 dp — a 24-unit drawing squeezed to 13 dp of art. At that width the A's crossbar and the 文's dot are single pixels, and the pair reads as “A with something after it” rather than as two characters.

It survives, but it is at the edge. Sixteen or seventeen dp — still inside the 22 dp line the label occupies — buys back a whole pixel of stroke and is the cheaper half of this change.

What I would build

01, at 16 dp, one colour, tinted at runtime like every other mark in the row — no second drawable for night, no accent in a heading that has none.

Implementation is one file: ic_sec_translate.xml redrawn with the A and the 文 inside one 24-unit box at 1.45 stroke, plus a 1 dp size bump in activity_settings.xml. Nothing else in the app refers to the old drawing.
