Night, reconsidered.

The dark page is not too dark because it is dark — it is too dark because the stair never rises: canvas sits at L* 5.5, the card above it at 15.8, and the three steps in between are 3 L* apart. Every surface is charcoal, the cream ink is 16.6:1 above it, and the whole page reads as one black field with white text laid on. Four ways to lift it without leaving the palette, four ways to settle the corner mark, and the transition rebuilt.

01

What is actually wrong

3.4

L* between canvas and band. The page has five surfaces — canvas, band, shell, card, chip — and they are 3.4, 2.9 and 4.0 apart. At that spacing a card edge is a suggestion; on the light page the same relationship is carried by a 12 L* paper-to-taupe step.

16.6:1

Cream ink on charcoal. Higher contrast than the light theme's 15.9:1, on a surface that is darker than the ink is bright. Cream belongs on paper; on a near-black page it stops reading as ink and starts reading as light.

L* 5.5

The canvas itself. Below L* 8 an OLED page stops being a page and becomes an absence — fine for a media player, wrong for a utility you look at while another app is behind it.

02

Four nights

Same roles, same cream, same clay — only the values move. Each card is the home page rendered in that palette; the measurements underneath are the ones the theme would be shipped on.

03

The corner mark

The launcher icon is a cream tile with the lens burned into it. On paper it is the brand; on charcoal it is a lamp. Four ways to keep the mark and lose the glare — shown on the lifted night canvas from 02.

04

The switch

Two layers, or one

The old switch faded the old page out and the new page in at the same time, over 190 ms. Both layers half-transparent means the window background shows through them: at the midpoint the whole screen is a blend of paper, charcoal and nothing. That dip is the blink.

The new switch fades one layer. The rebuilt page is complete from its first frame; the captured old page dissolves over it for 420 ms on an ease-in-out, so every frame is an honest blend of the two pages.

  • Duration190 ms → 420 ms, measured as a 345–420 ms ramp on the emulator.
  • Layerstwo fading against each other → one fading over a finished page.
  • Curvedefault decelerate → cubic-bezier(.38,0,.26,1).
  • Interferencethe setup sheet fired 350 ms into the rebuild → only on a real open.
  • Guardthe held frame could be recycled at 450 ms → 1 200 ms.
Recommendation — Umber, with the quiet tile and the one-layer dissolve. Contrast measured with the WCAG 2.1 formula; L* from relative luminance.
