Settings · translation

From and into, side by side.

Three directions for the same control: one glance shows the pair, one tap changes either side, and the arrow in the middle is the direction the words travel. Each option keeps the app's parchment and clay, and each is buildable from the layouts already in the app — no new image assets.

AThe split card
TRANSLATION
From tap one

Tap either half · the ring swaps them

Calmest of the three. One card, one hairline, one seam — it reads as a single decision with two ends rather than two settings.

  • Each half is a 150 dp tap target, up from a 28 dp chip.
  • The swap ring is the only new control; it hides while From is on Auto.
  • The half sinks for 240 ms before the picker opens — the press is seen first.

BThe greeting tiles
TRANSLATION

A word from each language, so the pair is recognised before it is read

Most readable at a glance. Each side shows the language speaking for itself — Hallo, Hello, こんにちは — which works for scripts a name can't carry, and needs no flags.

  • Needs one greeting string per language, kept beside the name.
  • Long greetings (Tiếng Việt, Bahasa) shrink to the name only.

CThe code plates
TRANSLATION

Two letters, one direction — the fastest to scan, dullest to look at

Most compact. Departure-board logic: the code is the identity, the ribbon is the direction, and the whole thing survives 20 languages of translation without breaking.

  • Smallest of the three — frees height for the permission card.
  • Auto reads as AUTO with a scan glyph.

In every option

From stays on Auto recognize by default and shows the same glyph wherever a language's identity would sit — ◎ with a small ripple. The picker and the lens keep working exactly as they do now; only the row's body changes.

What it costs

A and C are pure layout: one new drawable for the swap ring or ribbon, plus two click targets. B adds a greeting per language to Languages.kt, which is data, not markup.

Tap targets

Today's chips are 28 dp tall. All three make the whole side of the card the target — 64–76 dp tall — which is the change that makes this easier to change on a phone with one hand.
