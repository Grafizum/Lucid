Seven choices before the next build.

Everything the settings page asked for is built and on the phone. What is left is not more work — it is your call on seven forks, each with the current behaviour written down beside it. Pick one per card; the bar at the foot collects your answers as a line you can send back.

Pick the ones that are obvious; leave the rest for later.

Nothing here is undone work — the settings page, the app switches, the folded history and the automatic return from Android's accessibility screen are all built, tested and on the phone. These seven are the places where two reasonable answers exist and only you can say which one Lucid should ship with.
