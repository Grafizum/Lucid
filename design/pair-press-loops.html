Four loops for pressing half a card.

Each card below plays its own press on repeat — press, hold, picker, release — so you can watch the whole beat instead of guessing at a still. Loop 01 is what the build does now; the other three are there if you want a heavier or a quieter hand.

Loops run on their own; nothing to click inside a card.

In the build

What changed for loop 01

The feedback is no longer a rectangle laid over the half. half_press_from and half_press_into now carry the card's own 16 dp on the three edges that are the card's, and are square on the seam side, so the tint runs into the split and is cut by the seam line instead of floating beside it. Their 1 dp ring is gone — that ring was the second edge you were seeing inside the card's own hairline.

The half no longer scales away from the card either: at 0.966 it left a rim of paper around the tint, which is what made the feedback look like a smaller box on the box. What moves now is the medallion (0.94), the name's colour, and the tint's own fade — 120 ms down, 220 ms held, 400 ms back up, with the picker arriving inside the hold.

And the press was nearly invisible: a 74 % beige over paper measured 1.15 : 1. It is a solid step of the same beige now — 1.30 : 1 by day, 1.51 : 1 at night — so the state is carried by the surface, not by an outline.
