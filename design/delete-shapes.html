the way out

Five shapes to poke out of the bottom edge

All five are centred, symmetrical, and sized in dp at a 390 dp device width. Left pane is the mark at rest — the only thing on screen when nothing is happening. Right pane is the same shape once the lens is pushed all the way down, with the cross and the word inside it, sitting clear of the gesture bar. Pick by number or by name.

01

Half disc

The plainest geometry there is: a true semicircle whose flat side is the screen edge. It reads as a button rather than an ornament, and there is nothing to decode. The safest of the five.
